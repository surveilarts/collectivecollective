After the Signal

What shape does a presence leave behind? These six works move from the first stirrings of attention to the quiet persistence of an imprint. Each translates an invisible process into material: a pulse, a fold, a suspended trace.

Original procedural artworks and curatorial texts composed with AI for this exhibition. The sculptures are rendered live; no external artworks or artist attributions are implied.

How to explore

On a computer, drag the scene to look and use WASD to walk. On touch screens, drag to look and choose a work from the menu. The arrow buttons take you to a comfortable viewing point.

With a compatible headset, open this page over HTTPS and choose Enter VR. Aim either controller at a numbered floor marker and pull the trigger to teleport. Without controllers, look at a marker for a moment. Labels are also present inside the gallery. Use your headset's system control to leave VR.

Motion can be paused at any time. Reduced-motion preferences pause the sculptures automatically. Teleportation is immediate in VR.
