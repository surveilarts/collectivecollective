The Chromatic Conservatory

An imagined conservatory where mathematics learns the extravagance of living things. Six sculptures borrow the growth patterns of flowers, shells, coral and reefs, then unfold them in enamel, silk, pearl and light. Above them, a suspended corolla turns inside a ribbed glass pavilion.

Original procedural artworks and curatorial texts composed with AI for this exhibition. The sculptures are original mathematical forms rendered live; no external artworks or artist attributions are implied.

How to explore

On a computer, drag the scene to look and use WASD to walk. On touch screens, drag to look and choose a work from the menu. The arrow buttons take you to a comfortable viewing point.

With a compatible headset, open this page over HTTPS and choose Enter VR. Aim either controller at a numbered floor marker and pull the trigger to teleport. Without controllers, look at a marker for a moment. Labels are also present inside the gallery. The floating pause control near the central pool toggles animation. After teleporting in VR, turn toward the nearby sculpture. Use your headset's system control to leave VR.

Motion can be paused at any time. Reduced-motion preferences pause the sculptures automatically. Teleportation is immediate in VR.
