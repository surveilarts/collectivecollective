The Keeper’s Garden

A walled garden in the late-afternoon light. Its beauty comes from care: roses planted in repeated colors, herbs clipped to precise outlines, fruit branches trained along wires, and narrow gravel paths kept clear between the beds. Six planted rooms lead toward a climbing-rose arbor and a quiet place to sit.

An original garden designed and modeled with AI. The plants are stylized botanical forms, arranged as a tended landscape rather than a botanical identification guide.

How to explore

On a computer, drag the scene to look and use WASD to walk. On touch screens, drag to look and choose a garden room from the menu. The arrow buttons take you to a comfortable viewing point.

With a compatible headset, open this page over HTTPS and choose Enter VR. Aim either controller at a numbered floor marker and pull the trigger to teleport. Without controllers, look at a marker for a moment. Each garden room has a small plant-bed sign. The pause control beneath the far arbor toggles the butterflies and water. After teleporting in VR, turn toward the adjacent garden bed. Use your headset's system control to leave VR.

Motion can be paused at any time. Reduced-motion preferences pause the butterflies and water automatically. Teleportation is immediate in VR.
